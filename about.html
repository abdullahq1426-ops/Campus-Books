About Campus Books

Students often have textbooks left over after a course. Other students need those same books for the next semester.

Campus Books collects used book listings in one place. Each listing shows the course, price and condition, so students can compare books before choosing one.

For example, a student taking CPIS 358 can check the catalogue for related books and compare their prices instead of checking several class messages.

How to find a book | Book checklist

How it works

  1. Find your course in the catalogue.
  2. Choose a book and check its details.
  3. Compare its price and condition with the other copies.
View the catalogue

Before choosing a book

  • Check that the title and edition match your instructor's request.
  • Make sure the book is suitable for your course.
  • Ask about missing pages, notes or damage.
  • Compare the price with other available copies.
